# R4: Cross-chain movement and liquidity mechanics (simulator research)

Research input for the Stablecoins topic simulators: (1) liquidity control room, (2) cross-ledger failure lab. This is evidence for authors, not publishable copy.

- Prepared 18 Nov 2026. Sources accessed 17 to 18 Nov 2026. Post-June-2026 facts come only from live sources and carry their dates.
- Source order: protocol docs, contract source and issuer terms first, then independent incident analyses, then press. Vendor statements are labelled "vendor claim".
- Three PDFs (Circle Q3 2026 call transcript, LayerZero KelpDAO report, Circle access-denial policy) did not parse in the fetch tool. Their text was extracted locally from the downloaded files and read directly.
- Confidence: High = primary source read. Med = secondary source, partial primary, or a vendor metric whose meaning is unclear. Low = weak single source or inference without direct evidence.
- In section 3, [S] = sourced, [I] = analyst inference.

## 1. Editor summary

1. Circle CCTP V2 is the reference model for native burn/mint. The destination mint is replay-protected and safe to retry. The source burn is not: a second burn is a second transfer (XCH-11, XCH-12).
2. Fast Transfer is finality credit extended by Circle. A global allowance backs early mints until source burns reach hard finality. At zero, Fast is unavailable. Circle does not document who absorbs a reorganised burn (XCH-04, XCH-05).
3. CCTP V1 is mid-shutdown: burn limits cut from 31 Oct 2026, contracts paused 1 Dec 2026. Simulators should model V2 only (XCH-08).
4. "Stuck" CCTP transfers are recoverable while the burn exists. Attestations expire after about 24 hours, and re-attestation has no deadline (XCH-13).
5. Standard CCTP from L2s waits for L1 finality (about 15 to 19 min). Linea takes 6 to 32 h and Starknet 2 to 4 h. Fast takes about 8 to 20 s. Standard is free and Fast carries a variable fee (XCH-03, XCH-06).
6. CPN: Circle's operating entity says it holds no funds and is not a party. OFIs and BFIs settle directly. CPN Managed Payments is different: Circle holds assets and may extend credit (XCH-18, XCH-22).
7. CPN's documented state machine (CREATED, CRYPTO_FUNDS_PENDING, FIAT_PAYMENT_INITIATED, COMPLETED/FAILED, plus refunds and RFIs) can drive simulator (d) almost directly (XCH-21).
8. CPN volume figures are run-rates: $41B annualised (trailing 30 days) at end-Q3 2026, over $58B by 30 Oct, 205 FIs enrolled. These are vendor metrics, not audited flows (XCH-24).
9. A wrapped token is a claim on bridge collateral and on the honesty of the verifier set. Ronin, Wormhole, Nomad and Multichain show collateral loss. KelpDAO (Apr 2026, about $292M, 1-of-1 verifier) shows configuration risk persists (XCH-33 to XCH-40).
10. Cite Chainalysis correctly. Bridge attacks were 69% of 2022 thefts at mid-year. For the full year, bridges were 64% of the $3.1B DeFi total (XCH-41, XCH-42).
11. USDT0 locks USDT on Ethereum and burns/mints across other chains under a 3-of-3 DVN set. Contracts are upgradeable and carry a blocklist. The operator entity is not named in its docs (XCH-28 to XCH-30).
12. Finality copy must be dated. Ethereum takes about 15 min. "safe" is not "finalized". Solana's Alpenglow is reported live since 21 Oct 2026, under which Confirmed equals Finalized. Optimistic rollup exits still take about 7 days (XCH-44 to XCH-53).
13. Fiat rails bind, not chains. Fedwire runs business days with a cut-off. Circle Standard redemption takes up to 2 business days, while Express costs 5 bps for near-instant processing. Tether has a $100k minimum, a fee of at least $1,000, and "several days". Instant rails exist with limits (XCH-54 to XCH-60).
14. No issuer was found minting on credit. Just-in-time liquidity comes from prefunded issuer balances, instant rails, Circle Match hourly netting and third-party credit, and bank lines still have a weekend gap (XCH-57, XCH-64, XCH-67).
15. Freezes behave differently. A USDC transfer to a blocked address reverts. Ethereum USDT lands and is then frozen. A CCTP mint to a blocked recipient should revert (inference). GENIUS rules would require block/freeze/reject capability by 18 Jan 2027 at the latest (XCH-72 to XCH-75).

## 3. State machines for the simulator

"Retry-safe" means repeating the step cannot create a second economic transfer. Bank-credit points are analyst inference. Technical finality is not legal settlement finality, which depends on contract, scheme rules and jurisdiction.

### 3a. Native burn/mint (Circle CCTP V2)

| State | Entered when | Sender knows | Retry-safe? | Exits and failures | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A0 Instructed | Instruction accepted, customer amount moved to "outbound in transit" | Nothing on-chain | Yes, with an API idempotency key | Cancel, compliance reject | [I] |
| A1 Burn broadcast | depositForBurn (or WithHook) signed and sent | Tx hash only. It may be pending, dropped or replaced | No. A second depositForBurn is a second transfer. Replace using the same account nonce, or wait | Dropped or replaced: back to A0. Fee-switch chain with fee above maxFee: revert, nothing burned | [S] revert rule, [I] rest |
| A2 Burned, awaiting threshold | Burn receipt exists. API returns 404 until observed, then `pending` | Burn on-chain, no attestation | Poll only (40 req/s cap, 5-min lockout) | Source reorg before attestation: back to A1 [I]. Fast allowance at zero: waits for hard finality [I] | [S]/[I] |
| A3 Attested | API `complete` with message and attestation. expirationBlock about 24 h ahead | Everything needed to mint. Anyone can relay if destinationCaller = 0 | Yes | Expiry: A3x | [S] |
| A3x Attestation expired | Destination block passes expirationBlock | Same as A3 | Yes: POST /v2/reattest/{nonce}, no deadline | Back to A3 | [S] |
| A4 Mint broadcast | receiveMessage sent on destination | Destination tx hash | Yes. Idempotent, with nonce replay protection | Revert: A5 | [S] |
| A5 Mint reverted | Gas, wrong caller, wrong contract, missing Solana token account, expired attestation. Also a blacklisted recipient [I] | Revert reason | Yes once the cause is fixed. Nonce still unused | A3 or A3x. A blocked recipient waits for unblock [I] | [S]/[I] |
| A6 Minted | Mint succeeds, nonce marked used, USDC (net of any Fast fee) at mintRecipient | Destination receipt | Repeats revert with "Nonce already used" | A hook fails separately and the mint stands [I] | [S] |
| A7 Destination final | Destination chain meets the bank's confirmation rule | Final technical state | n/a | n/a | [I] |

Special paths. A V1 burn can still be attested and minted until V1 contracts pause on 1 Dec 2026. Circle says pending redemptions will be processed before the pause [S]. A Fast burn is attested at "confirmed" level against the allowance [S]. Who absorbs a later source reorg is undocumented [I].

When the bank may credit [I]. The beneficiary bank credits at A6 plus its own destination-chain rule (A7). For a Fast transfer, source-chain finality stops mattering to the recipient once the mint is destination-final, because Circle's allowance stood behind the early mint (Med). The sending bank closes "in transit" at A6, not at A2.

| B2 Locked or burned on source | Native token held by the bridge contract. Wrapped or OFT-mesh token burned | Receipt. Collateral is now under bridge control | n/a | Reorg before verifier quorum: back to B1 [I] | [S] |

Protocol-level states (not per transfer) [I]:
- P1, path credit exhausted: one-way flow drains a destination, and sends there fail until the AI Planning Module reallocates credit.
- P2, drained chain: LP withdrawals on that chain are constrained.
- P3, pool exploit: LPs and Hydra holders absorb the loss.

When the bank may credit [I]. At C5 plus C6. On pool chains the asset received is the issuer's native token. On Hydra chains it is a pool-backed OFT, so treat it as in 3b.

| State | Entered when | Sender (OFI) knows | Retry-safe? | Exits and failures | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D0 Quote selected | OFI requested quotes, CPN aggregated BFI quotes, FX locked | Rate and BFI | Yes, with an idempotency key | Quote expiry: undocumented | [S]/unknown |

| D2 CRYPTO_FUNDS_PENDING | BFI approved. OFI should create the on-chain tx | Amount and destination | Create once, with an idempotency key | n/a | [S] |
| D3 Tx CREATED, PENDING, BROADCASTED | OFI signed. CPN verified and broadcast | Tx status | Do not sign a second transfer until the first is FAILED or proven never broadcast [I] | Tx FAILED. Retry rule undocumented | [S]/[I] |

When the bank may credit [I]. As OFI, debit the sender into in-transit at D1 or D2. Close it at D6, or reverse it when the refund is COMPLETED. As BFI, credit the receiver when the local rail settles, not at D4. The USDC received at D4 becomes the BFI's inventory. Circle (CTS) says it is not a party [S], so OFI-BFI exposure from D4 to D6 is bilateral [I].

### 3e. Reconciliation and outcome-unknown overlay (all models)

| Record | Reference source | Typical break | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chain state, per chain | Own node plus an independent RPC provider | Dropped or replaced tx, reorg, false RPC answers (KelpDAO RPC poisoning, XCH-33) | Treat a single-RPC answer as unconfirmed. Need two independent sources to close in-transit [I] |
| Attestation/verifier record | Circle Iris API, Wormhole VAA, LayerZero status | Attested but not minted, expired | Re-attest, retry the mint [S] |
| Provider status | CPN webhooks/API, custody platform statuses | Lag, repeated "Completed" events (XCH-69) | Key on provider id plus tx hash. Consumers must be idempotent [S]/[I] |
| Issuer account | Circle Mint, Tether, Paxos balance | Unsettled wire, returned payout credited back (XCH-55) | Suspense until settled [S]/[I] |
| Fiat bank account | Bank statements | Missed cut-off, returns | Match on end-to-end reference [I] |
| Treasury and customer ledgers | Bank general ledger | Leg posted without its counter-leg | One in-transit account per leg, aged and escalated [I] |

Outcome-unknown rule [I]. A step is UNKNOWN when a broadcast or API call times out without a definitive answer. Resolve it by querying the tx hash, nonce or idempotency key. Never resend a value-creating step (burn, lock, pool send, CPN signature).

Safe resends:
- CCTP receiveMessage
- LayerZero lzReceive retries
- re-attestation requests
- API calls repeated with the same idempotency key

## 5. Myths and open questions

### Myths to avoid in copy and simulator labels

1. "CCTP is a bridge with pools." It burns and mints. The dependency is Circle's attestation service, not pooled liquidity (XCH-01).
2. "Fast Transfer means the source is final." It means Circle has extended finality credit through the allowance (XCH-04, XCH-05).
3. "Bridged USDC is USDC." A wrapped token is a claim on bridge collateral and on its verifiers (XCH-33 to XCH-35).
4. "Retrying is always safe." Mint retries are safe. Burns, locks, pool sends and new CPN signatures are not (XCH-11, XCH-12).
5. "On-chain confirmation is settlement." Technical finality varies by chain and can stall (XCH-46, XCH-47). Legal settlement finality is a separate question.
6. "Stablecoins run 24/7, so liquidity is 24/7." Primary-market and payout legs inherit rail windows (XCH-54 to XCH-56, XCH-67, XCH-68).
7. "A transfer to a blacklisted address always fails." It does for USDC. Ethereum USDT lands and is then frozen (XCH-72, XCH-74).
8. "On-chain volume measures payments." Q3 2026 USDC on-chain volume was $13.9T, against CPN's $41B annualised run-rate (XCH-58, XCH-24). The McKinsey/Artemis figure of about $390B in 2025 "real payments" was seen only in secondary sources.
9. "Circle is the counterparty in CPN." CTS says it is not a party and holds no funds. Managed Payments is the exception (XCH-18, XCH-22).
10. "Bridge risk was a 2022 problem." KelpDAO lost about $292M in April 2026 (XCH-33).
11. "Solana finality is about 12.8 s." That is outdated if Alpenglow is live (XCH-48, XCH-49).
12. "Moving USDC off an optimistic rollup takes 7 days." Native CCTP waits for L1 batch finality, not the challenge window (XCH-50, XCH-52).

### Open questions (not verified)

- CPN: quote expiry, the rule when the on-chain tx fails or lands late, and BFI prefunding and settlement-risk terms. The CPN Rules are not public.
- CCTP: the live Fast allowance size, Circle's treatment of a fast-attested burn that is later reorganised, the exact Fast fee table (it did not render), and whether V2 allows a recipient change before mint.
- Circle Mint: daily cut-off time, holiday calendar, and limits on RTP, SEPA Instant and PIX. Circle's redemption-structure help page did not load.
- Paxos and RLUSD: minimums, hours and cut-offs are unpublished.
- USDT0: the operator entity, the redemption path, and whether its blocklist checks recipients.
- Solana: official confirmation of the Alpenglow activation date (solana.com returned 429), and whether Circle's Solana timings reflect it.
- Bridge losses: 2025 and 2026 aggregates conflict. Hyperbridge (Apr 2026), Shibarium (Sep 2025), Garden Finance and others appear in search snippets only.
- GENIUS: final rules and whether the effective date moves earlier than 18 Jan 2027.
- Noble: a Circle Mint halt on 13 Oct 2026 and a full pause on 12 Jan 2027 appear in secondary snippets only.
- Netting: no primary source was found on bilateral netting between payout partners.
