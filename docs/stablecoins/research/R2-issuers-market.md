# R2: Stablecoin issuers, bank instruments and market size (as at 17–18 Nov 2026)

Prepared 18 Nov 2026 for the Stablecoins topic rebuild. Research input only: nothing here is site copy.

**Method.** Primary sources first: issuer terms, attestation PDFs (text extracted locally where WebFetch returned binary), regulator documents, and the DefiLlama API for supply. Press is used for dates and context. Issuer and partner statements are tagged **vendor claim** even when published on an issuer's own site.

**Conventions.**
- Supply is DefiLlama `circulating` as fetched on 17 Nov 2026 (intraday, so it moves during the day). Totals cover USD-pegged coins only.
- `[ISS-nn]` points to the claims register in section 3.
- "Search summary" means the fact was seen only in a search-result summary. The page itself was not opened, so confidence is capped at Med.
- Facts from before June 2026 that were not re-fetched are labelled "pre-cutoff knowledge".

## 1. Editor summary

1. **The site overstates market size.** DefiLlama shows USD-pegged stablecoins at **$286.4bn on 17 Nov 2026**. That is down from a **$312.9bn high on 17 May 2026** and from $303.0bn at end-2025. The site's "$320B+" is wrong, and so is "$210B to $320B+ in H1 2026": supply was flat in H1 2026 and has fallen about $19bn since 7 Oct [ISS-01, ISS-02, ISS-78].
2. **Shares on 17 Nov 2026:** USDT $175.2bn (61.2%) and USDC $69.2bn (24.2%). USDT's share has fallen from 67.8% at end-2024. USDC's rose from 21.6% and has stayed near 25% since mid-2025 [ISS-04, ISS-05].
3. **Open USD went live on 30 Sep 2026, not 12 Nov.** The operator's own blog, the issuer (Bridge) and Bloomberg all date go-live to 30 Sep. The "12 November 2026" opening appears only on openusd.inc, which neither Bridge nor joinopenstandard.com links to. Treat that date and the site's "$468M" figure as unverified [ISS-48, ISS-55].
4. **OUSD issuer and licence.** OUSD is issued by Bridge Building Inc., a Stripe subsidiary licensed as a US state money transmitter. Bridge National Trust Bank has only preliminary conditional OCC approval (12 Feb 2026) and is "not yet operational". Reserves are held at BNY, BlackRock and Lead Bank. No attestation firm or report was found [ISS-49, ISS-50, ISS-53].
5. **OUSD supply can be checked independently, but its other figures cannot.** DefiLlama shows about $961m, matching Bridge's own $960m, up 16% in a month. Partner counts (140+ at announcement, 200+ now), reward payouts and payment volumes are self-reported or unpublished [ISS-54, ISS-51].
6. **Every element of the site's Open USD description is stale.** "Announced" should be "live". The founders are Coinbase, Mastercard, Shopify, Stripe and Visa, and Stripe (whose subsidiary issues the token) is missing from the site. The cited Reuters article could not be found. "Shared reserve economics" is the operator's stated design, with no fee or payout data published [ISS-45 to ISS-53].
7. **How OUSD differs from USDG.** Both pass reserve income to distributors. OUSD splits the roles: a US issuer (Bridge) issues for a partner-governed operator that offers board seats and equity. USDG is issued by Paxos from Singapore (MAS) and Finland (FIN-FSA), and the Global Dollar Network (GDN) is a Paxos partner programme paying "up to 100%" of returns on balances held on partner platforms. Mastercard is in both [ISS-39 to ISS-42, ISS-51].
8. **PYUSD is issued by Paxos, not PayPal.** Paxos's PYUSD page says: "issued by Paxos, not PayPal or Venmo". Paxos says it now operates as Paxos Trust Company, N.A. under the OCC, but an August 2026 law-firm note says final approval was still pending. Resolve this before publishing [ISS-28, ISS-29].
9. **The site's Tether facts are stale.**
   - The issuer is Tether International, S.A. de C.V. (El Salvador), not a BVI company.
   - Tether says KPMG U.S. gave an unqualified audit opinion on its 2025 financial statements (13 Aug 2026), so "never completed a full audit" is now wrong.
   - H1 2024 profit was $5.2bn, not $6.2bn [ISS-19, ISS-21, ISS-23].
10. **USAT is live but small.** It launched on 27 Jan 2026 with Anchorage Digital Bank as issuer and stands at $179m [ISS-24, ISS-26].
11. **RLUSD.** It is issued by Ripple's NYDFS trust company, which Ripple's 16 Nov terms name "Ripple Custody & Trust Co." and October press calls Standard Custody & Trust Company. Ripple says Ripple National Trust Bank received final OCC approval on 26 Oct 2026; it will hold reserves, not issue. RLUSD runs on 8 networks with $2.79bn in circulation. The SEC case ended in Aug 2025, not with a 2024 settlement [ISS-33 to ISS-38].
12. **Circle.**
    - Deloitte & Touche LLP still examines USDC reserves monthly. The September report, dated 26 Oct 2026, shows $73.9bn USDC, with 84% of reserves in the BlackRock-managed Circle Reserve Fund.
    - No redemption minimum is published, so the site's "$100 minimum" is unsupported.
    - Circle National Trust (final OCC approval 10 Jul 2026) provides custody and does not issue USDC [ISS-07, ISS-08, ISS-10, ISS-14].
13. **Deposit tokens versus bank-issued stablecoins.**
    - JPMorgan describes JPM Coin as a "deposit token" that is "not a cryptocurrency or a stablecoin". It runs on Ethereum and Base, for vetted counterparties only.
    - Citi Token Services is live in seven markets and HSBC's Tokenised Deposit Service in five.
    - Bank-issued stablecoins now exist: SoFiUSD ($377m) and SG-FORGE's EURCV (€186m). They are bank or bank-subsidiary liabilities, but they are not deposits and holders are not insured.
    - The site's deposit-token matrix wrongly includes HSBC Orion and SG-FORGE [ISS-68 to ISS-73].
14. **GBP and EUR.**
    - No sterling issuer is authorised under the new UK regime. The FCA regime starts on 25 Oct 2027, and the BoE has set a £40bn per-coin guardrail. The sterling coins already circulating are tiny: the largest, tGBP, is £26m.
    - EURC (€514m) leads in euros. The 37-bank Qivalis consortium received its Dutch EMI licence on 14 Oct 2026 but is not issuing yet [ISS-59 to ISS-67].
15. **US regime dates.** The OCC finalised its GENIUS Act rule on 21 Oct 2026, effective 18 Jan 2027. Any "GENIUS-aligned" claim should carry a date [ISS-74].

### 2c. Deposit token, bank-issued stablecoin and non-bank stablecoin

| Question | Deposit token (JPM Coin, Citi TS, HSBC TDS) | Bank-issued stablecoin (SoFiUSD; SG-FORGE EURCV/USDCV) | Non-bank stablecoin (USDC, USDT, OUSD, USDG) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whose liability? | The bank's deposit liability. JPMorgan calls it a "bank-issued deposit token" [ISS-68] | A bank's (SoFi Bank, N.A.) or a bank subsidiary's (SG-FORGE, an EMI) liability, but as a stablecoin or e-money token, not a deposit [ISS-73, ISS-59] | The non-bank issuer's liability (e.g. Circle Internet Financial, LLC; Bridge Building Inc.) [ISS-07, ISS-49] |
| What backs it? | The bank's whole balance sheet, under capital and liquidity rules. The deposit can fund lending (inference) | A segregated 1:1 reserve held for holders [ISS-73, ISS-59] | A segregated 1:1 reserve [ISS-08, ISS-53] |
| Deposit insurance? | Yes, as a deposit, up to the per-depositor limit ($250k in the US). The FDIC proposed on 9 Apr 2026 that tokenised deposits remain deposits. Large institutional balances are mostly above the limit (inference) [ISS-72] | No. The FDIC proposal says reserves held as deposits get no pass-through cover for holders [ISS-72] | No [ISS-10, ISS-55] |
| Who can hold it? | Only the bank's onboarded clients: "vetted, approved counterparties" for JPM Coin, even on public chains; eligible corporates and FIs for Citi and HSBC [ISS-68, ISS-70, ISS-71] | Anyone the token reaches on a public chain, subject to issuer controls [ISS-73, ISS-59] | As bank-issued. Only onboarded customers can redeem directly [ISS-10, ISS-22] |
| Interest to holders | Possible, as with any deposit (not stated on JPMorgan's page) | Issuers may not pay interest under GENIUS or MiCA [ISS-75] | Same, but platforms pay "rewards" (PayPal, GDN, Open Standard) [ISS-31, ISS-41, ISS-52] |

## 5. What remains unproven

- **Open USD on 12 Nov 2026.** Whether there was a real operator milestone that day is unproven. Only openusd.inc says so, and its ownership could not be tied to Open Standard. The same site's "$468M" figure conflicts with Bridge and DefiLlama (about $961m). No press coverage of a 12 Nov event was found.
- **Open USD economics.** The management fee, the formula for rewards "proportional to supply and activity", the equity terms and any actual payouts are unpublished. The Forbes claim of "$1bn seed liquidity" could not be read.
- **Open USD assurance and scope.** No independent attestation has been located. It is unknown whether issuance will move to Bridge National Trust Bank or an NYDFS trust company, and whether OUSD is offered in the EEA.
- **Network revenue sharing in practice.** No issuer reports partner payouts by programme. Circle's $409m Q3 distribution cost is the only hard figure. The GDN "up to 100%" and Open Standard "nearly all" claims have no outcome data. USDG fell 13% and PYUSD 16% in the month to 17 Nov, and the reasons were not researched.
- **Settled versus announced volume.** Circle's "$13.9tn on-chain volume" and Citi's "billions of dollars" are self-reported and undefined. No product publishes a payments-versus-trading split. The third-party estimates (McKinsey/Artemis, Allium, BCG) were not read.
- **Tether.** The KPMG audit report itself was not read. The gold and bitcoin amounts come only from search summaries. MiCA status and USAT's redemption terms and economics were not checked.
- **Paxos charter.** It is unresolved whether OCC conversion is final (Paxos says it operates as an N.A.; DWT said final approval was pending in August). PYUSD's attestation firm and current reward rate were not verified.
- **RLUSD.** The issuer's legal name (Ripple Custody & Trust Co. versus Standard Custody & Trust Company) is unconfirmed against the NYDFS register. The OCC's final letter for Ripple National Trust Bank was not found.
- **Deposit tokens.** Neither JPMorgan's page nor HSBC's release states FDIC coverage or interest. JPME and further JPM Coin chains are unconfirmed. The FDIC tokenised-deposit rule is still a proposal.
- **Bank-issued stablecoins.** SoFiUSD's reserve location, attestation and post-GENIUS legal structure are unverified. SG-FORGE supply figures differ between sources.
- **Market drivers.** The causes of the roughly $19bn fall in supply since 7 Oct 2026, including USDC's drop from $74.1bn (31 Oct) to $69.2bn (17 Nov), were not researched. Other aggregators may show different totals.
- **GBP and EUR.** Qivalis's launch date, token name, chains and full member list are unknown. No sterling issuer is authorised under the new FCA regime.
